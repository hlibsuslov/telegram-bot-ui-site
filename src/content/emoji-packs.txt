# A line "## Category name" puts the links below it in that category (packs above any category go to "Other").
# Within a category, put the most popular packs first: the library's Popular row follows this order.
# Empty lines and other lines starting with # are ignored; duplicates are dropped.
# The site reads each pack through the emoji resolver: a pack Telegram does not know is skipped.
# Optional search tags go in emoji-tags.txt.
